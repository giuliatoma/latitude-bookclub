Silent Book Rave

A monthly reading night with an ambient DJ, in your bookstore.

Guests bring a book, settle into cushions and read in silence for an hour while a DJ plays soft ambient music. Then they talk, and browse your shelves. The format began with Jucy Book Rave in Tokyo and has since spread to bookshops in other cities.

20minutes to settle in
60minutes of silent reading, ambient music at low volume
30minutes to talk and browse the shelves

About 110 minutes on a weekday evening, for 25–30 guests who buy tickets in advance. Monthly, or as a single trial edition.

What your bookstore gets

  • New customers. An audience of readers, drawn to your shop by the event.
  • Book sales. The last 30 minutes are built around your shelves, with guests already in the mood to buy.
  • Extra sales. Anything guests order beyond the included drink is yours.
  • Your costs covered. Part of every ticket goes to you for the included drink and the extra staff hours. We agree the amount together.
  • Visibility. Photos and posts that tag your shop, and an audience that follows the event.
  • A regular event. A monthly slot that builds a returning crowd.

What we’d ask of you

  • The space for about 2.5 hours on an agreed weekday evening, with room for 25–30 people on cushions or low seating.
  • A team member on site for the evening.
  • A welcome drink for each guest, such as tea, included in the ticket.
  • Power for a DJ setup, and a quiet corner near the shelves.
  • Open shelves during the final 30 minutes.
  • A quick check that your music licence covers a DJ set at a ticketed event, or can be registered for the night.

Let’s talk for 15 minutes, or meet at the space and find a date.
